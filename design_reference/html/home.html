Who We Are

About Us

Energicotel (ECTL) PLC is a publicly listed energy company established in 2014 in Rwanda as a subsidiary of the EPC Africa Group. With over a decade of experience, ECTL is recognized for its innovation, resilience, and excellence in the energy sector. The company operates as both an Independent Power Producer (IPP) and an Engineering Consulting Firm, delivering reliable power generation, advanced engineering solutions, and sustainable energy projects that support Rwanda's national development and regional energy ambitions.

Hydro Power Generation

Leading Rwanda's renewable energy sector with state-of-the-art hydroelectric facilities that provide clean, reliable power to the national grid.

  • Nkora Hydro Power Plant
  • Cyimbili Hydro Power Plant
  • Lihanda HPP (Kenya) - Under Development

LPG Distribution & Services

Launching in 2026 with comprehensive LPG solutions for clean cooking and energy access, revolutionizing how families cook and businesses operate.

  • Fast Delivery Services
  • B2B & B2C Solutions
  • Clean Cooking Initiatives

ECTL Engineering

Engineering & Advisory

Providing expert engineering consultancy for major infrastructure projects across the region, leveraging our technical expertise and industry partnerships.

  • Rusizi Port Substation
  • Partnership with AECOM Canada
  • Infrastructure Development Advisory
